Instrument samples (MP3)

Folders: piano, flute, clarinet, saxophone, bassoon, horn, trumpet, trombone, tuba,
violin, cello, contrabass. Only a selection of notes of each instrument is included;
the app computes the notes in between. The viola has no samples of its own: it uses
cello samples for low notes and violin samples for high notes.

Source: tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
(folder "horn" = "french-horn" there)
Original recordings: Versilian Studios Orchestra 2 (VSO2) and others, see the source.
